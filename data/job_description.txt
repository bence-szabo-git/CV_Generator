About

About the Group 

The Procurement Group (PG) leads UNOPS’ effort to enable public procurement to be a catalyst for sustainable development. Based in Copenhagen but connecting all parts of the organization that contribute to sustainable procurement, we articulate the organization’s sustainable procurement practice strategy and provide thought leadership to position UNOPS with its partners. We foster innovative solutions for responsible supply chains, manage critical data and systems, drive digital innovation, develop systems to support effective purchasing, and drive our suppliers’ responsibility. Furthermore, PG is actively developing capacities for data-driven decision-making and foresight to enhance procurement outcomes. PG enables the operationalisation of the public procurement principles. It aims to bridge the gap between policy and implementation, and supports UNOPS teams worldwide through strategic advice, organization-wide knowledge-sharing, capacity building, insightful reporting and managerial oversight of UNOPS procurement activities. We promote procurement that not only delivers value for money, fairness and transparency, but also empowers institutions, supports local economies, and reduces environmental impact. Working across sectors and contexts, PG helps ensure that public procurement is used as a powerful lever to drive equitable development and long-term impact.

 Role Purpose 

The Procurement Intern – Data & Analytics will support the Procurement Group’s efforts to strengthen data-driven decision-making by improving the accessibility, quality, and usability of procurement data. The role contributes to the development of analytics products, dashboards, and automation/AI-supported workflows that provide timely insights into procurement performance, compliance, risks, and opportunities. Through this work, the intern will support continuous improvement in how procurement data is collected, analyzed, visualized, and used across the organization.

Upon completion of this internship, the Procurement Intern – Data & Analytics will have gained practical experience and knowledge in the following areas:

Data Management: Understanding of data collection, cleaning, validation, and consolidation techniques from various sources.
Data Analysis: Ability to identify trends, patterns, and variations in procurement data and contribute to the interpretation of findings.
Data Visualization: Proficiency in developing and maintaining dashboards and reports using business intelligence/visualization tools.
Digital Tools and Process Improvement: Exposure to the development and testing of data tools, including automation and AI-enabled capabilities.
Research and Analytical Skills: Experience in conducting research projects related to procurement, supply chain, sustainability, data, and digital tools.
Coordination and Communication: Enhanced skills in project coordination, communication with internal and external partners, and preparation of training materials.

Functions / Key Results Expected

 Data management and quality
Assist in the collation, cleaning, validation, and consolidation of procurement data from different sources to support accurate and consistent analysis. Support documentation of data definitions, assumptions, and basic quality checks.
 Analysis and insights
Support analytical work to identify trends, patterns, and variations in procurement data and contribute to the interpretation and communication of findings for different audiences.
 Data visualization and reporting
Assist with the development and maintenance of dashboards, reports and other data products, including the development of integrated views of procurement information to highlight specific offices/projects that require management attention and targeted early interventions (i.e. through heatmaps based on available data points). Support the preparation of recurring communications and data outputs for internal and external audiences.
 Digital tools and process improvement
Support the development of data tools including responsible exploration and testing of automation or AI-enabled capabilities. Support the improvement of recurring data workflows and reporting processes (e.g. streamlining data preparation steps, improving templates, or testing new features/tools).
 Research and analysis
Support research projects (e.g. data collection, desk research, market scanning), including trends and innovations related to procurement, supply chain, sustainability, data, and digital tools, as well as internal improvements.
 Coordination support
Provide support in project coordination with internal and external partners. Support training events/initiatives including set-up, logistics, materials preparation, and implementation.
