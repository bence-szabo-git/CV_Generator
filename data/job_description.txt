 Your Role 

We are looking for a Junior Data Scientist who will support our manufacturing operations with insights gained from analysing shop floor data and that will be part of a data science team on development projects, typically involving large, complex data sets.

The candidate will perform all activities associated with the design, prototype, and development of learning models and act as a facilitator between the technical and the shopfloor team, collecting information and supporting the implementation and maintenance of data science solutions. He/She will join the Business Process Innovation team whose purpose is to guide the innovation inside Luxottica manufacturing all over the world.

Main Responsibilities

 Support the Data Science team to identify new opportunities to drive business solutions forward. 
 Support the shopfloor team on current and future AI solutions. 
 Explore, mine and analyse data from company and internal databases. 
 Assess the effectiveness and accuracy of new data sources and data gathering techniques. 
 Co-develop custom data models and algorithms to apply to data sets. 
 Use predictive modelling to increase process quality. 

Main Requirements

 Strong knowledge of a variety of machine learning techniques (deep learning, graph neural networks, tree-based methods, SVM, PLS, PCA, clustering, etc.) and their real-world advantages/drawbacks. 
 Knowledge of Computer Vision techniques (image processing) and main algorithms (Convolutional Neural Networks, Autoencoders, …) 
 Strong knowledge of statistical computer languages (Python, SQL). Experience with PyTorch is a plus. 
 Proven Experience with Azure and Databricks Environment is a plus. 
 Knowledge of advanced statistical techniques and concepts (regression, properties of distributions, statistical tests and proper usage, etc.) and experience with applications. 
 Experience working with large and complex datasets from manufacturing or industrial environments is a plus. 
 Experience working with BI software (Power BI) is a plus.
