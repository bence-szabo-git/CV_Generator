About the Opportunity
Within Driscoll’s, the Nursery is responsible for the production and supply of plant material to our growers. At our own nursery in Helenaveen, we produce over 40 million plants annually across 18 different sites, supplying our grower network throughout Europe and Africa.
To ensure we deliver high-quality plants on time and in the right volumes, access to accurate and reliable data is essential. As a Nursery Intern, you will play a key role in this process.
Responsibilities
As an intern, you will support the Nursery team with data collection, analysis, and reporting. You will contribute to improving our processes and help drive the transition toward a data- and technology-driven organization.

Your responsibilities include:

Collecting cultivation data and maintaining performance dashboards

Analyzing propagation (production) results

Forecasting production output

Creating daily and weekly performance reports

Designing new dashboards and performance metrics

Aligning with the Nursery team on current and future data needs

Providing recommendations to improve data collection and reporting, including the use of AI

Supporting the rollout of performance metrics across the European Nursery organization

Candidate Profile
You are an enthusiastic and analytical student with an interest in data, technology, and horticultural processes.

Additionally:

You are pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in horticulture, supply chain, business administration, or a related field
You are in your 3rd or 4th year
You have strong communication skills and are a team player
You have a hands-on mindset and strong analytical skills
You have an interest in data, technology, and production processes
You are fluent in English, both written and spoken
You have a valid driver’s license and your own transportation
What do we offer?
A 5–6 month internship within an international organization
The opportunity to contribute to the development of our Nursery operations
Hands-on experience with data, technology, and process improvement
A dynamic and supportive team environment
Broad international exposure and future career opportunities
Your primary work location will be Helenaveen, with occasional meetings at our European headquarters in Breda.

About Driscolls
Driscoll's is the global market leader for fresh strawberries, blueberries, raspberries and blackberries. With more than 100 years of farming heritage and hundreds of independent growers around the world, Driscoll's is passionate about growing fresh, beautiful and delicious berries. Our values of humility, passion and trustworthiness have guided our mission to delight consumers around the world. Driscoll's exclusive patented berry varieties are developed through years of research using only natural breeding methods – meaning, no GMOs. From farm-to-table, we focus on delivering a high quality, premium berry experience with our many supply chain partners. Driscoll's is the trusted brand for Only the Finest Berries™.
