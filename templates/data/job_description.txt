Responsibilities

Make an impact

Contribute to CI/CD pipelines (build, lint, test, quality gates) under mentorship; add checks and maintain pipeline health,
Implement or extend API/unit/smoke/performance tests for critical scenarios; keep test data and environments tidy,
Help maintain Sonar and other quality tools; fix or triage issues to keep quality gates green,
Build small internal tools/CLI scripts/dashboards that reduce developer overhead (e.g., test scaffolding, report generation),
Partner with engineers across teams to understand needs and propose automation or tooling improvements,
Monitor and report quality/throughput trends (dashboards, simple queries) to support data‑driven decisions,
Work in Agile/Scrum rituals; use Git effectively (PR hygiene, branching),
Research modern AI‑based developer tools and quality‑automation solutions under guidance.
